FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Messenger DM Tool?

It's a Google Chrome extension that lets you send promotional and outreach messages — like business promos, lead generation, or hashtag campaigns — to your Facebook friends, groups, followers, and target audiences automatically. Just type your message once, click Start, and let it run in the background.

Does it work on Mac and PC?

Yes. It runs in Google Chrome on both macOS and Windows, in the background, all day — even while you sleep.

Can I target specific audiences?

Yes. Target by hashtag, location, post, custom user lists, group members, followers, or following.

Is there a free version?

Yes, there's a free version with a daily sending limit. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited daily messages and advanced features.

What is the Pro version?

Pro unlocks unlimited daily DMs, more bots and templates, image messages, blacklist, hyper-personalized texts, and import/export of bots, templates, and history.

Can I send images or media?

Yes — sending image messages is a Pro feature.

What is spin syntax?

Spin syntax like {Hi|Hello} {name} lets the bot randomly vary your message so each DM feels personal and unique.

Will my account get banned?

The tool includes smart pacing, configurable time intervals, pauses, and a blacklist to reduce risk. Use responsibly.

Does it work if Facebook is in another language?

For best compatibility, switch Facebook's language to English and restart the task.

Can I pause or stop a campaign?

Yes. Pause or stop at any time. The task also auto-pauses if several consecutive targets fail.

Can I export or import my setup?

Yes (Pro). Export/import DM bots, templates, and task history as JSON/CSV.
